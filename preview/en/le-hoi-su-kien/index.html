Festivals and events

Dates follow the lunar calendar and may change according to local announcements.

  1. 3rd day of the Lunar New Year (1st lunar month)Usually late January or February
    Traditional rite revived in 2024

    Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine Rite

    The spring rite of Ngọc Vừng island village, honouring the three Phạm brothers, venerated as guardian deities for helping Trần Khánh Dư in the 1288 Battle of Vân Đồn, and praying for national peace and favourable weather. Lapsed through historical upheavals, it was revived on the third day of Tết in the Year of the Dragon, 2024; according to the Quảng Ninh newspaper, the procession of the deities had not been held for more than 60 years. The rite features a procession from the shrine to the communal house with flag bearers, a traditional ensemble and a dragon palanquin carried by young men, followed by drumming, bell strikes, incense offerings and the reading of a prayer.

    Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine, Ngọc Vừng island — Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine
  2. 3rd day of the 3rd lunar monthUsually March or April

    Mother Goddess Anniversary at Quan Lạn's Đền Mẫu

    The Holy Mother's anniversary is held every year at the Eastern Gate Mother Temple on Quan Lạn island, a temple to the Three Mother Goddesses that still keeps a royal decree of 1889. Before 1959, about two months after the Quan Lạn rowing festival, Yến Hải village also held a boat race on a loop of about 1,600 m from Đền Mẫu round Trấn Hải Temple; the race has not been held since 1960 and has not yet been revived.

    Đền Mẫu, eastern gate, Quan Lạn island — Quan Lạn Mother Goddess Temple (Đền Mẫu)
  3. 10th–19th day of the 6th lunar month; main day on the 18thUsually falls in July (in 2025 the main day was 12 July)
    National Intangible Cultural Heritage (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, 20 December 2019)

    Quan Lạn Communal House Festival (Vân Đồn Traditional Festival)

    Quan Lạn island's biggest festival of the year, commemorating the 1288 Vân Đồn victory, when Trần forces under Prince Nhân Huệ Vương Trần Khánh Dư destroyed the Yuan general Trương Văn Hổ's supply fleet on the Mang River, and praying for the safety of those who live by the water. It runs from the 10th to the 19th day of the 6th lunar month, with the main day on the 18th.

    The rites include processions of royal decrees, offerings at the communal house, pagoda, shrines and nghè, the “thu rầm” and “phong tướng” (conferring of generals) rituals and a parade on the Mang River; the tablet of the guardian deity Trần Khánh Dư is carried from the nghè to the communal house and back at the end. The highlight is the afternoon rowing race on the main day between the Đông Nam Văn and Đoài Bắc Võ teams: beforehand the two sides parade round the village and meet three times in the communal-house courtyard, symbolising the Trần's three victories over the Yuan, and their two “generals” make offerings at Đức Ông Shrine before setting off. On 20 December 2019 the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism inscribed the festival on the National List of Intangible Cultural Heritage.

    Quan Lạn Communal House, Đình landing and the Mang River, Quan Lạn island — Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex, Quan Lạn Relic Cluster: Sao Ỏn, Đông Hồ and Cao Sơn Shrines
