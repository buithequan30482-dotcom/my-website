Personal Data Processing Policy

1. Legal basis and scope

This policy is based on the Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree No. 356/2025/NĐ-CP, effective from 1 January 2026. It applies to personal data you provide when submitting comments, feedback or photos through the forms on the Vân Đồn Special Zone heritage website.

2. Data controller

The Culture and Society Office of Vân Đồn Special Zone (Vân Đồn Special Zone People's Committee, Quảng Ninh province) determines the purposes and means of processing personal data collected through this website.

3. Data we collect

  • Display name (required): your real name or a nickname, shown publicly with your comment.
  • Email address or phone number (optional): used only to contact you if needed; never shown publicly.
  • The content of your comment or feedback.
  • Photos you upload (optional).
  • Minimal technical data: we do not store your original IP address; the system keeps only a hash (one-way encoding) of it to prevent spam and duplicate submissions. The hash is not used to identify you.

Please do not include sensitive information about yourself or others in comments or photos (identity document numbers, health information, images of other people without their consent, etc.).

4. Purposes of processing

  • Displaying your comments and photos on the relevant heritage page (after moderation);
  • Receiving feedback to supplement or correct information about heritage sites;
  • Contacting you to clarify your feedback (if you provided an email address or phone number);
  • Preventing spam, unlawful content and abuse of the forms.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer it to third parties for commercial purposes. Data is disclosed to competent state authorities only where the law requires.

5. Consent

By ticking the consent box and submitting the form, you agree to the processing of your data under this policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it. Children submitting comments need the consent of a parent or guardian as required by law.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies or third-party behavioural tracking tools.

7. Retention period

Personal data linked to comments and feedback is kept for no more than 24 months from submission. After that it is anonymised: display names, email addresses and phone numbers are deleted, and the comment text may be kept in a form that no longer identifies the sender. Comments rejected in moderation or deleted on request are removed sooner.

8. Your rights

Under personal data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of your personal data;
  • Request to view and access your personal data;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your data, or removal of comments or photos you submitted;
  • Withdraw consent, object to or request restriction of processing;
  • Lodge complaints or denunciations in accordance with the law.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the address below with enough information to identify your comment (display name, time of submission, heritage page). We will respond within the time limits set by law.

9. Data security

We apply appropriate organisational and technical measures to protect your data: access is limited to assigned staff, data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and original IP addresses are not stored. If a personal data breach is detected, we will handle and report it as required by law.

10. Contact

  • Culture and Society Office, Vân Đồn Special Zone
  • Address: Vân Đồn Special Zone People's Committee, Quảng Ninh province
  • Phone: being updated
  • Email: being updated

11. Policy updates

This policy may be revised when the law changes or new website features are added. The new version will be posted on this page with its update date.
