Uncle Hồ Memorial Site (Vạn Yên)

Thôn Vạn Yên 2, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Báo Quốc tế (ảnh: Quân chủng Hải quân cung cấp)

A memorial site in the former Vạn Yên commune, recorded in the heritage inventory as connected with a visit by President Hồ Chí Minh and built in 1962.

The Uncle Hồ Memorial Site ("Uncle Hồ" is the affectionate Vietnamese name for President Hồ Chí Minh) is in the former Vạn Yên commune (now Vạn Yên 2 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 1,000 m². The heritage list records only: "Built in 1962. Uncle Hồ visited."

No official published record describing President Hồ Chí Minh's visit to Vạn Yên has been found yet. Published articles on Hồ Chí Minh sites in Vân Đồn mainly refer to the President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island (a national monument). This page will be updated once the inventory dossier and verification by the competent authorities are available.

President Hồ Chí Minh's visit to Vạn Hoa naval port (13 November 1962)

Articles in Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2013, 2019, 2023) record that on his seventh visit to Quảng Ninh, on 13 November 1962, President Hồ Chí Minh visited Ngọc Vừng island and Vạn Hoa naval port. According to an archival photo caption in Quảng Ninh Newspaper (8 September 2019), Vạn Hoa naval port now lies on Cái Bầu island; according to a study by Prof. Nguyễn Văn Kim (VNU Journal of Science, 2016), Vạn Hoa port is located in Vạn Yên commune. The visit to Vạn Hoa is therefore most likely the event behind the heritage list's note "Uncle Hồ visited"; this still needs to be checked against the original inventory dossier.

According to Nhân Dân and Báo Quốc tế (19 May 2026), this was the third time he visited the Navy: he called on Unit 135 at Vạn Hoa – today Squadron 135, Brigade 170, Naval Region 1. He told the officers and sailors the story of General Trần Khánh Dư, who used a stratagem to destroy the Yuan supply fleet – a battle tied to the Vân Đồn waters – and urged them: "As navy soldiers, you must love the island as your own home", working hard to turn it into a place both prosperous and beautiful. Quảng Ninh Newspaper has also published archival photos of him talking with navy personnel at Vạn Hoa and posing with the crew of the ship Hải Lâm.

Visitor guide

The site's location and condition have not been identified and there is no visitor information. Visitors interested in President Hồ Chí Minh's connection with Vân Đồn can go to the President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island.
