Provincial Monument

Khe Mai Resistance Base

Thôn Đoàn Kết 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Photo of the surrounding area · Chuyên trang Du lịch Quảng Ninh (Diên Khánh - Nguyên Ngọc)

A provincial historical monument in Đoàn Kết 1 hamlet, recorded in the inventory as a revolutionary base of the district Party organisation; listed in 2015. Detailed information is being compiled.

Introduction

The Khe Mai Resistance Base lies in the former Đoàn Kết commune, now Đoàn Kết 1 hamlet of Vân Đồn Special Zone, in the northern part of Cái Bầu island. On 9 February 2015 the Quảng Ninh People's Committee issued Decision No. 402/QĐ-UBND listing it as a provincial historical monument. The site covers about 83,000 m², and the inventory records its condition as good.

History

According to the provincial heritage inventory, Khe Mai was a revolutionary base of the Vân Đồn district Party organisation. Note that the area's administrative names have changed several times: Cẩm Phả district was established on 26 December 1948 with 12 communes; on 13 March 1994 it was renamed Vân Đồn district, with the Cô Tô archipelago separated into a district of its own; and from 1 July 2025 all 12 communes and towns of the district merged to form Vân Đồn Special Zone. Historical records from the resistance years therefore usually refer to the district as Cẩm Phả.

During the resistance years, the islands of Vân Đồn were on the front line of the north-eastern seas. Records on Ngọc Vừng island commune note that after 1946 many places in the area were cut off from Hòn Gai and placed under War Zone III, returning to the Hòn Gai Special Zone only in December 1948. In that context, the secluded forested hills of Cái Bầu island were important for the activities of resistance forces.

Significance

As one of the special zone's 11 listed monuments, the Khe Mai Resistance Base marks the revolutionary struggle of the local Party organisation and people. It complements Vân Đồn's revolutionary heritage sites, alongside the President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site and the 12.7 mm anti-aircraft position on Ngọc Vừng island, and has educational value for younger generations.

Note on sources

At the time of writing, no published online material has been found describing the base's period of activity, the bodies stationed there or the events associated with Khe Mai. Details will be added from the listing dossier and the history of the local Party organisation. Readers with documents or memories to share are welcome to contribute through the comments section.

The relic should be distinguished from present-day places that share its name. According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (article of 1 October 2023), Khe Mai is also the name of a hamlet in the former Đoàn Kết commune, home to the Khe Mai farm eco-site of more than 10 ha, about 500 m from Vân Đồn Airport. According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (article of 18 June 2025), the site is run by the Khe Mai Xanh Cooperative as an agritourism destination. It is a modern tourism business, not the Khe Mai Resistance Base relic, and these articles do not mention the base. How Khe Mai hamlet relates to the relic area (listed in the inventory as Đoàn Kết 1 hamlet) has not been confirmed; the Culture and Social Affairs Office of the special zone should check this against the classification dossier.

Visitor guide

The site is in Đoàn Kết 1 hamlet in the north of Cái Bầu island, reachable by road from central Cái Rồng. The map location is approximate and there is no information on visitor facilities; please contact the Special Zone's Culture and Society Office or the hamlet authorities in advance for directions.
