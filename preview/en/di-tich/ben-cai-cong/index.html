Cái Cổng Landing

Thôn Minh Châu, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Photo of the surrounding area · Báo Quảng Ninh (bài: Xuân Hòa, 13/09/2022)

The remains of an ancient landing of the Vân Đồn trading port system in Minh Châu, dated by the heritage inventory to the Lý dynasty.

OverviewLink with the Vân Đồn trading port PhotosReviews

Overview

Cái Cổng Landing is in the former Minh Châu commune (now Minh Châu village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 1,000 m². The heritage list describes it as an ancient landing of the Vân Đồn trading port, dating from the Lý dynasty.

Link with the Vân Đồn trading port

The Vân Đồn trading port was established in 1149 under King Lý Anh Tông; it was not a single harbour but a system of landing places spread across many islands of Bái Tử Long Bay. Cái Cổng is one of the ancient landings of this system, but it is not among the components of the Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex (VD01) that was classified as a Special National Monument in 2023.

No archaeological publication or official article dedicated to Cái Cổng has been found, and its location on the ground has not yet been identified in the project's data.

When reporting on Prime Ministerial Decision No. 1225/QĐ-TTg (2023), which ranked the Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex as a Special National Monument, Báo Văn Hóa (24 October 2023) and Sài Gòn Giải Phóng (25 October 2023) described the port as a network of wharves spread over roughly 200 km² and named them in pairs by commune: Cống Đông and Cống Tây (Thắng Lợi); Cái Làng and Cống Cái (Quan Lạn); Cái Cổng and Con Quy (Minh Châu); Cống Yên and Cống Hẹp (Ngọc Vừng). National newspapers thus record Cái Cổng as one of the two ancient wharves in former Minh Châu commune. However, these articles describe the port's wharf system in general; they do not publish the list of sites or the protection-zone maps attached to the decision.

According to Báo Quảng Ninh (5 May 2024), Quan Lạn island covers about 15 km² and is about 20 km long but very narrow in places; it comprises two communes, Quan Lạn and Minh Châu, and is regarded as the centre of the old Vân Đồn trading port. Cái Cổng wharf lies on the Minh Châu part of the island, in the same landscape as the Cống Cái, Cái Làng and Con Quy wharves.

Visitor guide

The site is in Minh Châu village on Quan Lạn Island, but its exact location has not been identified and there are no visitor facilities.

This is a ruin with no visitor facilities or on-site guiding. Please do not dig or collect artefacts (ceramic sherds, bricks, stones, etc.) – all artefacts at heritage sites are protected by law.
