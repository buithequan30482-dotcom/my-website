National Monument

President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island

Thôn Ngọc Vừng, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Báo Sài Gòn Giải Phóng (ảnh: Phạm Thơ)

Commemorates President Hồ Chí Minh's visit to the soldiers and people of Ngọc Vừng island in November 1962. The site comprises the helicopter landing ground and a memorial area with an exhibition hall, stele house and half-moon pond; it was listed as a national monument in 2023.

History

In November 1962 President Hồ Chí Minh visited the soldiers and people of Ngọc Vừng island. As to the exact date, the provincial heritage inventory and some archive photographs give 12 November 1962, while many other articles give 13 November 1962. He arrived by helicopter; as recalled in the Quảng Ninh newspaper, he stepped out smiling and waved to the islanders and soldiers.

During the visit he looked into the mess hall and kitchen just as the soldiers were preparing dinner, asked after the elderly and handed out sweets to the children. He urged the island's authorities and people to look after residents' health, educate the children and improve living standards, and told soldiers and civilians to stand united, help one another and build cooperatives, ready both to fight and to support the fighting.

Those words sustained Ngọc Vừng through the years of bombing that followed: the island commune endured thousands of bombs and rockets, shot down 23 American aircraft and was named a Hero of the People's Armed Forces in 1973. Also in 1962, General Secretary Trường Chinh visited the island, and the beach on its southern shore has borne his name ever since.

According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2018), the visit came as the island commune was beginning the first five-year plan (1961–1965); residents heard helicopters, saw two land safely, and a third arrived about an hour later. Sài Gòn Giải Phóng and Dân Việt (2022) report that President Hồ Chí Minh travelled with several leaders of the Party Central Committee; commune leaders were told one day in advance that a high-level delegation was coming, and everyone guessed it was Uncle Hồ without being sure. According to Sài Gòn Giải Phóng, besides the advice already mentioned, he urged islanders to grow rice and other crops and to fish in order to develop the economy, stressing that soldiers must help the people with production and construction.

According to the outline for the 60th anniversary of Quảng Ninh province, republished by Quảng Ninh Newspaper (23/10/2023), this was the seventh of his nine visits to Quảng Ninh; on this trip he visited both Ngọc Vừng island and Vạn Hoa naval port.

Witness accounts

According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (19/05/2018), several people present in 1962 still keep the memories: Ms Nguyễn Thị Lan (Bình Ngọc hamlet), later Party Secretary of the commune; young teacher Nguyễn Thị Ngọc Xuân; Mr Phạm Văn Biên, who told President Hồ about island life; and Nguyễn Minh Trang, the young pioneer team leader who clearly recited Uncle Hồ's Five Teachings when asked, and who later became Chairman of the Vân Đồn District People's Committee. He also led the children in singing "Em là thiếu niên tiền phong" and "Kết đoàn".

According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (27/01/2025), Ms Ngọc Xuân, then a teacher and deputy pioneer leader at the primary school, recalled that teachers and pupils ran towards the army unit on hearing the helicopter; when she faltered in starting the song, he stood up and conducted himself. He told them: "Teachers must teach well. Pupils must be diligent and good." The 1962 photograph of him with Ngọc Vừng children was provided by Mr Nguyễn Cảnh Loan (Hạ Long City) and found by Colonel Nguyễn Quang Vinh; according to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2018), Ms Xuân had searched for it for nearly 60 years.

Listing

The site was listed as a provincial monument in 2008. On 24 February 2023 the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism issued Decision No. 401/QĐ-BVHTTDL designating the President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island a national monument. The demarcated area exceeds 36,450 m², including nearly 26,780 m² in protection zone I and nearly 9,671 m² in zone II.

According to an article by the provincial Department of Home Affairs published in Quảng Ninh Newspaper (04/12/2013), the spot where he spoke to soldiers and residents was ranked a provincial-level relic in 2008; in 2010 the Provincial People's Committee decided to restore and expand the site, including a stele house marking where he stood and a protected zone where the aircraft landed; on 20/9/2013 the work received a plaque celebrating the 50th anniversary of the province. The Quảng Ninh provincial portal (2019) notes the site was restored and expanded in 2012–2013, while Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2021) reports that after three investment phases it covered more than 2.5 ha.

Layout and landscape

The site has two main areas. The first is the helicopter landing ground where Hồ Chí Minh first set foot on the island. The second contains the memorial buildings: an entrance gate, an exhibition hall, a stele house, a half-moon pond, gardens and service buildings. A 2016 article in the Quảng Ninh newspaper noted the landing ground and two banyan trees, one of them planted by the President.

After renovation and expansion, the memorial area lies on Võ Nguyên Giáp Road in Bình Ngọc hamlet, right in the centre of the island commune, with a planned area of about 6.3 ha, of which more than 2.4 ha is built. Beyond the gate is a half-moon pond of about 1,300 m² where visitors can release fish; from the pond one looks toward the stele house and the banyan tree under which, according to a newspaper caption, the President spoke with the islanders in 1962. The green, open grounds feel like a small park in the heart of the island village.

As for the banyan trees, according to Phụ nữ mới (03/06/2026) the site actually has two: one is where he stood to speak to soldiers and residents, the other he planted himself before leaving the island. Dân Việt and Sài Gòn Giải Phóng (2022) describe the tree he planted as standing to the left of the memorial area, shading the yard in front of the memorial house, its trunk dividing into six or seven large branches; islanders and soldiers built a garden and a commemorative stele at its foot. According to VietnamPlus (2026), the half-moon pond now holds hundreds of goldfish.

Significance

The memorial site records Hồ Chí Minh's affection for the people of the maritime frontier and is a regular venue for heritage education for military units, schools and organisations. Visitors may hear local residents, sometimes eyewitnesses themselves, recount the day the President came. The site is also a common starting point for exploring Ngọc Vừng, continuing to Trường Chinh beach, the old citadel, the anti-aircraft position on Điếm Canh hill and the flag tower.

Commemorations and visitor information

According to Dân Việt (17/08/2022), every year on 12 November the island's soldiers and residents hold a rally and offer incense in his memory at the foot of the memorial banyan tree. According to Phụ nữ mới (2026), the memorial site is open 24 hours a day, charges no admission and has no fixed visiting hours.

Getting there: Sài Gòn Giải Phóng (2022) reports a drive of more than 2 hours from Hà Nội to Vũng Đục port (Cẩm Phả), then over an hour by boat to the wharf on the north of the island; according to Dân Việt (2024), speedboats take about 30–40 minutes and wooden boats about 1.5 hours; the best season is April to September, avoiding storms. Sài Gòn Giải Phóng (2022) estimated the island receives about 8,000–10,000 visitors a year.

On 12 February 2026, during a visit to bring Tết gifts to the armed forces on Ngọc Vừng, a provincial delegation led by Vice Chairman of the Provincial People's Committee Nguyễn Văn Công offered incense at the Hồ Chí Minh memorial site on the island (Báo Quảng Ninh, 12 February 2026).

Visitor guide

Take a passenger boat from Ao Tiên Port to Cống Yên landing on Ngọc Vừng; from there an electric cart takes about 15–20 minutes to the commune centre where the memorial stands. Boat schedules vary with season and weather; check with the port for the latest timetable.

This is a revolutionary heritage site: please dress respectfully, keep it clean and do not climb the memorial banyan tree or structures. Groups wishing for a guided talk should contact the local authorities in advance.
