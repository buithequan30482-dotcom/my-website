National Monument

President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island

Thôn Ngọc Vừng, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

Commemorates President Hồ Chí Minh's visit to the soldiers and people of Ngọc Vừng island in November 1962. The site comprises the helicopter landing ground and a memorial area with an exhibition hall, stele house and half-moon pond; it was listed as a national monument in 2023.

History

In November 1962 President Hồ Chí Minh visited the soldiers and people of Ngọc Vừng island. As to the exact date, the provincial heritage inventory and some archive photographs give 12 November 1962, while many other articles give 13 November 1962. He arrived by helicopter; as recalled in the Quảng Ninh newspaper, he stepped out smiling and waved to the islanders and soldiers.

During the visit he looked into the mess hall and kitchen just as the soldiers were preparing dinner, asked after the elderly and handed out sweets to the children. He urged the island's authorities and people to look after residents' health, educate the children and improve living standards, and told soldiers and civilians to stand united, help one another and build cooperatives, ready both to fight and to support the fighting.

Those words sustained Ngọc Vừng through the years of bombing that followed: the island commune endured thousands of bombs and rockets, shot down 23 American aircraft and was named a Hero of the People's Armed Forces in 1973. Also in 1962, General Secretary Trường Chinh visited the island, and the beach on its southern shore has borne his name ever since.

Listing

The site was listed as a provincial monument in 2008. On 24 February 2023 the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism issued Decision No. 401/QĐ-BVHTTDL designating the President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island a national monument. The demarcated area exceeds 36,450 m², including nearly 26,780 m² in protection zone I and nearly 9,671 m² in zone II.

Layout and landscape

The site has two main areas. The first is the helicopter landing ground where Hồ Chí Minh first set foot on the island. The second contains the memorial buildings: an entrance gate, an exhibition hall, a stele house, a half-moon pond, gardens and service buildings. A 2016 article in the Quảng Ninh newspaper noted the landing ground and two banyan trees, one of them planted by the President.

After renovation and expansion, the memorial area lies on Võ Nguyên Giáp Road in Bình Ngọc hamlet, right in the centre of the island commune, with a planned area of about 6.3 ha, of which more than 2.4 ha is built. Beyond the gate is a half-moon pond of about 1,300 m² where visitors can release fish; from the pond one looks toward the stele house and the banyan tree under which, according to a newspaper caption, the President spoke with the islanders in 1962. The green, open grounds feel like a small park in the heart of the island village.

Significance

The memorial site records Hồ Chí Minh's affection for the people of the maritime frontier and is a regular venue for heritage education for military units, schools and organisations. Visitors may hear local residents, sometimes eyewitnesses themselves, recount the day the President came. The site is also a common starting point for exploring Ngọc Vừng, continuing to Trường Chinh beach, the old citadel, the anti-aircraft position on Điếm Canh hill and the flag tower.

Visitor guide

Take a passenger boat from Ao Tiên Port to Cống Yên landing on Ngọc Vừng; from there an electric cart takes about 15–20 minutes to the commune centre where the memorial stands. Boat schedules vary with season and weather; check with the port for the latest timetable.

This is a revolutionary heritage site: please dress respectfully, keep it clean and do not climb the memorial banyan tree or structures. Groups wishing for a guided talk should contact the local authorities in advance.
