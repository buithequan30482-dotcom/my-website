Quan Lạn Beach

Thôn Quan Lạn 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Scenic Landscape
Mia.vn

Kilometres of fine white sand on Quan Lạn Island, close to a lively centre with a market, a walking street and plenty of accommodation.

LandscapeCharacterNearbyBest timeRecent activity PhotosReviews

Landscape

Quan Lạn Beach is in Quan Lạn 1 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone, and was inventoried as a scenic landscape in 1996, covering about 100,000 m². According to VOV, Quan Lạn's beaches stretch for kilometres with pure white, evenly fine quartz sand. The island has three main beaches – Quan Lạn, Minh Châu and Sơn Hào; Sơn Hào beach (former Quan Lạn commune) appeared on the province's 2018 list of tourist beaches, managed by Viglacera Vân Hải JSC.

According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2017), Sơn Hào beach on Quan Lạn island has a sandy strip about 3 km long with clear water, fringed by casuarina forest; it was the first beach on the island route recognised as meeting standards for tourist use, and is equipped with warning buoys, rescue boats and a standing team of 8 lifeguards. The travel site Mia.vn describes Quan Lạn beach as stretching for several kilometres, with stilt-style guesthouses set among willow and casuarina trees.

Character

Compared with Minh Châu, the Quan Lạn side is livelier, with a market, a walking street and many hotels, guesthouses and homestays, but its waves are stronger, the water murkier and there are more rocks (VnExpress, 2024). Visitors can watch the sunrise, go squid fishing and try the local speciality, peanut worms (sá sùng).

According to VnExpress (2013), of the island's beaches Quan Lạn beach is the closest to the port and the market, convenient for newly arrived visitors, while Minh Châu beach is farther away, quieter and has clearer water.

Nearby

  • Eo Gió (Windy Pass): about 5 km south of Quan Lạn port, near Cậu Temple and Cửa Đông; a 10–15-minute climb leads to views over the island and sea (wear flat shoes).
  • Historic sites: the Quan Lạn communal house, pagoda, shrines and temple, and components of the Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex.
  • Quan Lạn communal house festival: held in the middle of the 6th lunar month and famous for its rowing races; it has been listed as National Intangible Cultural Heritage since 2019.

Best time

Summer is the liveliest beach season on Quan Lạn; book accommodation and boat tickets early at peak times.

The travel site Mia.vn suggests roughly May to November as a good time to visit Quan Lạn beach. From Cái Rồng port the boat trip to the island takes about one hour, after which visitors can reach the beach by electric car or tuk-tuk.

Recent activity

According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (26 April 2026), during the 2026 Hùng Kings' Commemoration and 30 April–1 May holidays, room booking rates on Cô Tô, Quan Lạn and Minh Châu islands stayed at 90–95%; pedestrian streets in Quan Lạn and Minh Châu were scheduled to open on 1 and 2 May respectively. A visitor quoted by the paper said the island's roads and hotels had become more convenient, and that her child enjoyed swimming, catching snails and raking clams.

According to a Quảng Ninh TV report (July 2024), Quan Lạn island is promoted as an "all in one" seaside destination, with the 5-star Angsana Quan Lạn resort adding high-end leisure tourism.

Visitor guide

Take a high-speed island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (Bến cảng cao cấp Ao Tiên), the departure point for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023. Boats to Quan Lạn Island call at either Quan Lạn port or Minh Châu port, on opposite sides of the island about 12 km apart; electric shuttle cars and motorbike rentals are available on the island.

Swimming safety

  • Swim only in permitted areas with signage and lifeguards, and follow the beach management's instructions.
  • Do not swim far out, in high waves, strong wind, thunderstorms or at night; do not swim after drinking alcohol or right after a heavy meal.
  • Always watch children; non-swimmers should use a buoyancy aid.
  • Watch out for rocky reefs, sharp objects and marine creatures in shallow water.
  • Follow weather forecasts and local authority notices, especially in the storm season.

The Quan Lạn side has stronger waves and more rocks than Minh Châu – take extra care in the water, especially with children.

Check the boat schedule and weather forecast before setting out, especially in the rainy and storm season; services may be suspended in bad weather.
