Hà Vực Village Communal House

Thôn Đoàn Kết 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Photo of the surrounding area · Báo Quảng Ninh điện tử (bài: Duy Khoa)

The ruins of Hà Vực village's communal house (former Đoàn Kết commune), built in 1911 and dedicated to the village guardian deity; traces of the stepped foundation and some brick and tile fragments remain.

HistoryCurrent state PhotosReviews

History

Hà Vực Village Communal House (đình – the village hall and shrine) is in the former Đoàn Kết commune (now Đoàn Kết 1 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). According to the heritage list, it was built in 1911 to worship the village guardian deity (Thành hoàng) and served as the centre of village religious life.

Current state

Today the đình is a ruin that has not been restored; traces of its stepped foundation and some broken bricks and tiles can still be seen on site. It was inventoried in 2015 and covers about 1,000 m².

The remaining traces are a valuable record of Hà Vực village's history. Apart from the inventory list, no dedicated published source on the đình has been found.

Visitor guide

The site is in Đoàn Kết 1 village on Cái Bầu Island, reachable by road.

This is a ruin with no visitor facilities or on-site guiding. Please do not dig or collect artefacts (ceramic sherds, bricks, stones, etc.) – all artefacts at heritage sites are protected by law.
