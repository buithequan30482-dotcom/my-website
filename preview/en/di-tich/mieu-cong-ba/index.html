Cổng Bà Shrine

Thôn Minh Châu, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

A shrine of local folk belief in Minh Châu, recorded in the heritage inventory as dating from the Lý dynasty.

OverviewBeliefs PhotosReviews

Overview

Cổng Bà Shrine is in the former Minh Châu commune (now Minh Châu village, Vân Đồn Special Zone) on Quan Lạn Island. It is a historical site, inventoried in 1996, covering about 200 m².

Beliefs

According to the heritage list, the shrine dates from the Lý dynasty and belongs to local folk belief. The Lý dynasty was also when the Vân Đồn trading port was established (1149).

No published records yet describe the deity worshipped, the architecture or the rituals of the shrine; this page will be updated when the heritage dossier becomes available.

Visitor guide

The shrine's exact location has not yet been verified in the project's data; please ask local residents before visiting.

Take a high-speed island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (Bến cảng cao cấp Ao Tiên), the departure point for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023. Boats to Quan Lạn Island call at either Quan Lạn port or Minh Châu port, on opposite sides of the island about 12 km apart; electric shuttle cars and motorbike rentals are available on the island.

At places of worship, please dress modestly, keep quiet and respect local religious practices.
