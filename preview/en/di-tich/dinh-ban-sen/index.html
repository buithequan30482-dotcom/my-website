Bản Sen Communal House

Thôn Bản Sen 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

The communal house of the Bản Sen islanders, first built in 1925 and dedicated to the general Phạm Công Chính; it has been restored as a three-bay building.

HistoryWorshipSignificance PhotosReviews

History

According to the heritage list of Vân Đồn Special Zone, Bản Sen Communal House (đình – the village hall and shrine of a Vietnamese village) was first built in 1925 and has since been restored as a three-bay building. It is a historical site, inventoried in 1996, covering about 1,500 m², and now belongs to Bản Sen 1 village.

Worship

The đình is dedicated to the general Phạm Công Chính. In the Vân Đồn islands he is known as the eldest of the three Phạm brothers venerated by the people of Quan Lạn: Đức Ông Shrine honours Phạm Công Chính, Sao Ỏn Shrine Phạm Quý Công, and Đông Hồ Shrine Phạm Thuần Dụng. According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper, the three brothers died in the Battle of Vân Đồn – Cửa Lục in 1288 against the Mongol-Yuan invaders; the researcher Hoàng Giáp considers them deputy commanders of Trần Khánh Dư.

Significance

The đình is the spiritual centre of the island community and a link between Bản Sen and the network of sites honouring the Phạm generals across the Vân Đồn islands.

Detailed information on its architecture, artefacts and rituals has not yet been published in official sources; this page will be updated when the heritage dossier becomes available.

Visitor guide

Take an island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (the hub for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023) to Bản Sen. According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2015), motorbikes and tuk-tuks can be hired near the pier; accommodation and food services for visitors are limited, so plan ahead.

At places of worship, please dress modestly, keep quiet and respect local religious practices.

Check the boat schedule and weather forecast before setting out, especially in the rainy and storm season; services may be suspended in bad weather.
