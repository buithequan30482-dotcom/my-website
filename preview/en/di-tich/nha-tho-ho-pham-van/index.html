Phạm Văn Family Ancestral Hall

Thôn Quan Lạn 2, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

The ancestral hall of the Phạm Văn family on Quan Lạn Island, dated to the Nguyễn dynasty in the heritage inventory.

OverviewSignificance PhotosReviews

Overview

The Phạm Văn Family Ancestral Hall is in the former Quan Lạn commune (now Quan Lạn 2 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). According to the heritage list, it dates from the Nguyễn dynasty and is used for ancestor worship by the family; it was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 142.3 m².

Significance

An ancestral hall keeps a family's memory alive and binds its generations together. Alongside the communal house, pagoda, shrines and temples, family halls help shape the cultural character of the fishing village of Quan Lạn.

No published records yet describe the family's history or the hall's architecture and artefacts; this page will be updated when the heritage dossier becomes available.

For context, according to Pháp luật Việt Nam newspaper (2021), local history records that in 1288 the Trần army and people under Trần Khánh Dư, together with three brothers of the Phạm family, fought for ten days against the supply fleet of Trương Văn Hổ; a temple to the three Phạm brothers still exists on the island. VnExpress (2013) also notes that the Nghè shrine of Quan Lạn worships Mr Phạm Công Chính, a local man who died in the Battle of Vân Đồn. These sources show that the Phạm family holds a notable place in the history of Quan Lạn village; however, no published document yet confirms any link between the Phạm Văn ancestral hall and these figures.

Visitor guide

This is a private family ancestral hall; its location is not yet in the project's data. Visitors should contact family representatives or the village authorities in advance and behave respectfully. To reach Quan Lạn Island, take a high-speed ferry from Ao Tiên Port.
