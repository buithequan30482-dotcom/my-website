Minh Châu Village Communal House

Thôn Minh Châu, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

The communal house of the fishing village of Minh Châu on Quan Lạn Island, which received royal ordinations in 1887 and 1905; its original roof has been lost and a restoration campaign began in 2015.

HistoryWorshipRestorationSignificance PhotosReviews

History

Minh Châu Village Communal House (đình – the village hall and shrine) is in the former Minh Châu commune (now Minh Châu village, Vân Đồn Special Zone) and is where villagers gather for community affairs. According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2015), it was built nearly 200 years earlier. The "Thần tích Thần sắc" records kept at the Institute of Hán-Nôm Studies show that the đình received two royal ordinations: on the 1st day of the 7th month of the 2nd year of Đồng Khánh (1887) and on the 11th day of the 8th month of the 3rd year of Duy Tân (1905).

Worship

The sources differ. Quảng Ninh Newspaper says the đình worships the Holy Patriarch Nguyễn Minh Không, a master learned in Buddhist teaching who is revered as the patron of bronze casting. The heritage inventory list, however, dates the đình to the Trần dynasty and states that it worships the three Phạm brothers (Phạm Công Chính, Phạm Quý Công and Phạm Thuần Dụng) – generals widely venerated on Quan Lạn Island.

Restoration

Over the years the original roof was lost. In 2015 the Vietnam Elderly Charity Fund and the local community launched a campaign to rebuild the đình; the province instructed Vân Đồn district to work with Vietnam Maritime Corporation (Công ty CP Hàng hải Việt Nam) on the procedures.

Significance

The đình is a spiritual and cultural centre for the island people of Minh Châu. It was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 500 m². Visitors to Minh Châu beach can stop by to learn about the beliefs of this fishing village.

Visitor guide

Take a high-speed island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (Bến cảng cao cấp Ao Tiên), the departure point for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023. Boats to Quan Lạn Island call at either Quan Lạn port or Minh Châu port, on opposite sides of the island about 12 km apart; electric shuttle cars and motorbike rentals are available on the island.

At places of worship, please dress modestly, keep quiet and respect local religious practices.

Check the boat schedule and weather forecast before setting out, especially in the rainy and storm season; services may be suspended in bad weather.
