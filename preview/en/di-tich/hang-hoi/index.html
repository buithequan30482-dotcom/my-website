Hang Hoi Cave

Thôn Bản Sen 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Scenic Landscape
Photo of the surrounding area · Cổng TTĐT tỉnh Quảng Ninh – Chè Vân Bản Sen (Nguyễn Trang, 08/05/2024)

A cave in the Bản Sen area, inventoried in 1996. The heritage list records Stone Age traces and its use for storing food and ammunition during the resistance war against the United States.

OverviewSignificanceArchaeological finds PhotosReviews

Overview

Hang Hoi Cave is in the former commune of Bản Sen (now Bản Sen 1 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 1996 as a historical and scenic site, covering about 5,000 m².

Significance

According to the heritage list, the cave contains Stone Age traces. During the resistance war against the United States it was used to store food and ammunition – a reminder of the war years on Vân Đồn's island communes.

No scientific publication or official article dedicated to Hang Hoi has been found; the information above comes from the heritage inventory list.

Archaeological finds

According to Viet Nam News (VNS, November 2010), an archaeological team led by Professor Trịnh Năng Chung found traces of prehistoric habitation at Hoi Cave in Bản Sen commune, together with Áng Giá Cave in Thắng Lợi commune, during a survey of Bái Tử Long Bay. The finds included stone tools, fossilised burnt wood, animal bones and large numbers of shells of two kinds of snail: freshwater Melania and land-dwelling Cyclophorus; many of the freshwater shells had their tips broken off, probably to extract the meat. Two stone tools were also found in a stream bed and are thought to have been abandoned during tool-making.

The article adds that Professor Trịnh Năng Chung believed the occupants lived when the islands were still joined to the mainland, and that they may have belonged to the Soi Nhụ culture – previously identified in Hạ Long and Bái Tử Long bays – dating to about 10,000 years ago.

Visitor guide

Hang Hoi is not organised for visitors and its recorded location is approximate. Anyone wishing to visit should contact the local authorities and go with a resident who knows the terrain. To reach Bản Sen, take an island ferry from Ao Tiên Port.
