Minh Châu Beach

Thôn Minh Châu, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Scenic Landscape
Báo Quảng Ninh điện tử (bài: Hà Phong)

A crescent-shaped beach of fine white sand, clear water and a gently sloping shore on Quan Lạn Island, often ranked among the most beautiful beaches in northern Vietnam; old syzygium (trâm) trees line its edge.

Landscape

Minh Châu Beach is in Minh Châu village on Quan Lạn Island in Bái Tử Long Bay, and was inventoried as a scenic landscape in 1996 (about 20,000 m²). The crescent-shaped beach has fine white sand, clear calm water and a gently sloping shore; Quảng Ninh Newspaper describes it as one of the most beautiful beaches in northern Vietnam. It is one of the island's three main beaches, together with Quan Lạn and Sơn Hào, and remains fairly unspoilt, separated from neighbouring beaches by rocky headlands.

According to VnExpress (2013), Minh Châu is the quietest beach on the island because it lies apart from the others; its sand is firm and its water is rated the clearest among Quan Lạn's beaches. Because the beach faces the wind, debris from the sea sometimes washes ashore. About 2 km from the beach is Minh Châu port, where local fishing boats moor; the article describes it as the best spot on the island to watch the sunrise.

According to an article by Hà Phong (from the Quảng Ninh Provincial Portal, republished by the Vietnam National Authority of Tourism in 2023), the island commune of Minh Châu lies only about 3 m above sea level; early in the morning, visitors can follow locals to nearby Cồn Trụi beach to gather seafood.

Nearby highlights

  • Syzygium forest: the old trâm trees along the shore, which locals call the "sacred trees of Minh Châu"; the stretch of about 50 m at the start of the beach is a favourite photo spot.
  • Ninh Hải white dunes and the "river between white sand banks": on the road from Quan Lạn to Minh Châu, a lovely place to watch the sunset in the late afternoon.
  • Tắm Tiên (Robinson) beach: more than 1 km of white sand, with rocky reefs and bigger waves.

According to Công Thương (Industry and Trade) Magazine (March 2024), the trâm (Syzygium) forest on Minh Châu island is among Quảng Ninh's best-known groves of ancient trees: covering more than 3.4 ha, it comprises over one hundred trees estimated to be around 300 years old. Their interlocking canopies form a barrier against storms, blowing sand and waves for the settlement behind, and the trees once helped islanders through times of hunger.

Best time and experiences

Summer is the main beach season in Minh Châu. According to VnExpress the beach looks its best in the morning, while Quảng Ninh Newspaper suggests avoiding the midday sun and heading to the sea in the late afternoon. Visitors can stay in homestays, join locals digging for peanut worms (sá sùng), gather seafood at dawn or fish for cá đục in season; in 2022 the commune had more than 50 guesthouses and hotels.

According to the travel site Sun Paradise Land, Minh Châu beach lies in the northeast of the island, about 12 km from the Quan Lạn port area – roughly 15–20 minutes by motorbike or electric car. Because the water level changes with the tide, the width of the beach varies through the day; visitors are advised to swim in the morning or early afternoon and not to swim too far from shore.

Planning and tourism development

According to Nhadautu.vn (April 2021), the Quảng Ninh Provincial People's Committee recognised the Quan Lạn – Minh Châu tourist area, covering about 2,400 ha across the two island communes of Quan Lạn and Minh Châu, as a provincial-level tourist area. According to Báo Đấu thầu (February 2022), Decision No. 339 of the Provincial People's Committee approved an adjusted 1:2,000 zoning plan for the Minh Châu – Quan Lạn island area of about 4,360 ha, of which Minh Châu commune accounts for about 724 ha. The area is oriented towards high-end tourism linked to marine culture and ecology, while conserving its cultural and historical values.

Visitor guide

Take a high-speed island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (Bến cảng cao cấp Ao Tiên), the departure point for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023. Boats to Quan Lạn Island call at either Quan Lạn port or Minh Châu port, on opposite sides of the island about 12 km apart; electric shuttle cars and motorbike rentals are available on the island.

Swimming safety

  • Swim only in permitted areas with signage and lifeguards, and follow the beach management's instructions.
  • Do not swim far out, in high waves, strong wind, thunderstorms or at night; do not swim after drinking alcohol or right after a heavy meal.
  • Always watch children; non-swimmers should use a buoyancy aid.
  • Watch out for rocky reefs, sharp objects and marine creatures in shallow water.
  • Follow weather forecasts and local authority notices, especially in the storm season.

Tắm Tiên (Robinson) beach has rocky reefs and bigger waves – Quảng Ninh Newspaper advises going with local people for safety.

Check the boat schedule and weather forecast before setting out, especially in the rainy and storm season; services may be suspended in bad weather.
