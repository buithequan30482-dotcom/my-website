Chùa Trong Pagoda Ruins

Thôn Thắng Lợi, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Photo of the surrounding area · Báo Quảng Ninh – Thương cảng Vân Đồn qua các thời kỳ lịch sử (25/05/2013)

The ruins of a Trần-dynasty Buddhist pagoda in the former Thắng Lợi commune, in the islands of the Vân Đồn trading port system.

ContextLocation on Cống Tây Island PhotosReviews

Chùa Trong ("Inner Pagoda") is in the former Thắng Lợi commune (now Thắng Lợi village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 700 m². According to the heritage list, it was a Buddhist pagoda dating from the Trần dynasty and is now in ruins.

Context

The Thắng Lợi islands (Cống Đông – Cống Tây) were part of the Vân Đồn trading port system. Quảng Ninh Newspaper reports that Cống Tây island has traces of five pagodas and one tower from the Trần period, and that its shores are thick with Vietnamese and Chinese ceramic sherds from the 13th–17th centuries. Chùa Trong is not named individually among the components of the classified Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex (VD01).

No archaeological study dedicated to Chùa Trong has been published.

Location on Cống Tây Island

According to the article “Exploring Cống Đông and Cống Tây islands” in Quảng Ninh Newspaper (2012), Chùa Trong is one of five pagodas whose remains survive on Cống Tây island, alongside Lấm, Cát, Vụng Chuồng Bò and Vụng Cây Quéo pagodas.

According to a study by Đinh Thị Thanh Nga (Institute of Archaeology) in the journal Khảo cổ học (Archaeology), no. 2/2023, the eastern side of Cống Tây has five coves; the cove of hamlet 4 is also called Chùa Trong cove, and together with the cove of hamlet 5 (Cầu Cảng cove) it now belongs to Thi Đua hamlet. The author places Chùa Trong within the island's Trần-dynasty system of pagodas and towers, which includes the Bảo Tháp tower and the Lấm, Cát and Cây Quéo pagodas.

According to VietNamNet (2026), the pagodas on Cống Tây were all built on elevated ground overlooking sheltered coves where trading ships anchored; only the name and remains of Chùa Trong are known, with no detailed archaeological description yet.

According to Đinh Thị Thanh Nga (Institute of Archaeology) in the Journal of Archaeology no. 2/2023, the coves of Cống Tây island include Hamlet 4 cove (Chùa Trong cove). Quảng Ninh newspaper (08/08/2022) lists Trong pagoda (Thương Cảng Tự) among the pagodas on Thắng Lợi island proposed for restoration with social funding. Nhân Dân newspaper (27/11/2024), citing Prof. Nguyễn Văn Kim, names Trong pagoda together with Lấm, Hộ, Cát and Vụng Cây Quéo pagodas on Cống Đông and Cống Tây islands as evidence of maritime cultural and trade exchange.

Visitor guide

The former island commune of Thắng Lợi (now Thắng Lợi village) is served by passenger boats from Ao Tiên Port. Sites are scattered across the islands and around the coves, so ask the local authorities or residents for directions before setting out.

This is a ruin with no visitor facilities or on-site guiding. Please do not dig or collect artefacts (ceramic sherds, bricks, stones, etc.) – all artefacts at heritage sites are protected by law.
