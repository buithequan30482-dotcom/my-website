Vân Sơn Temple and Shrine

Thôn Quan Lạn 3, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Photo of the surrounding area · Báo Đại Đoàn Kết (ảnh: TTXVN)

A temple-and-shrine complex in Sơn Hào hamlet (Quan Lạn), dated to the Trần dynasty in the heritage inventory, dedicated to the Holy Mother, the three Phạm brothers and the Mountain God Cao Sơn.

OverviewBeliefsContext PhotosReviews

Overview

Vân Sơn Temple and Shrine are in Sơn Hào hamlet, former Quan Lạn commune (now Quan Lạn 3 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). The site was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 5,206.1 m²; the heritage list dates it to the Trần dynasty.

Beliefs

It is a complex with several objects of worship: a temple to the Holy Mother (Thánh Mẫu), worship of the three Phạm brothers, and a shrine to the Mountain God Cao Sơn. The three Phạm brothers (Phạm Công Chính, Phạm Quý Công and Phạm Thuần Dụng) are venerated by the people of Quan Lạn as generals who died in the Battle of Vân Đồn – Cửa Lục in 1288 against the Mongol-Yuan army. Quảng Ninh Newspaper also names Vân Sơn among Quan Lạn's notable heritage sites.

On the deity Cao Sơn, Báo Quảng Ninh (2020) records a local tale: he was one of the 50 sons who followed Lạc Long Quân to the sea, was skilled in medicine and often appeared as a village healer; when smallpox struck Quan Lạn one year, his help allowed the people to survive, so they built a shrine in gratitude. The article places this shrine, called Cao Sơn thần miếu, in Thái Hòa hamlet.

On the three Phạm brothers, Báo Quảng Ninh (2013) reports that, according to oral tradition, they led local militia alongside Trần Khánh Dư against the Yuan at Gót estuary and fell in battle; their bodies drifted ashore at three places, where villagers built three shrines: Đức Ông shrine (Đoài hamlet) for Phạm Công Chính, Sao Ỏn shrine (Tân Phong hamlet) for Phạm Quý Công, and Đông Hồ shrine (Thái Hòa hamlet) for Phạm Thuần Dụng. In 2011, Professor Hoàng Giáp and colleagues traced the three to Khê Chanh village (Quảng Yên). According to Báo Quảng Ninh (2023), the Quan Lạn boat-racing festival in their memory is held on the 18th day of the 6th lunar month each year.

Context

Sơn Hào has many traces of the Vân Đồn trading port – the Cống Cái – Sơn Hào archaeological zone has been studied by the Institute of Archaeology since the 1990s. A visit to Vân Sơn can be combined with the island's communal house, pagoda, shrines and trading port sites.

In mid-December 2017, workers building the Lòng Dinh reservoir water-supply works in Sơn Hào hamlet unearthed an old cannon bearing a Chinese-character inscription tentatively read as “Minh Mạng 18”. The artefact is kept at Vân Sơn temple (Báo Đại Đoàn Kết, citing VNA, 28/12/2017).

Visitor guide

Take a high-speed island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (Bến cảng cao cấp Ao Tiên), the departure point for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023. Boats to Quan Lạn Island call at either Quan Lạn port or Minh Châu port, on opposite sides of the island about 12 km apart; electric shuttle cars and motorbike rentals are available on the island.

At places of worship, please dress modestly, keep quiet and respect local religious practices.

Check the boat schedule and weather forecast before setting out, especially in the rainy and storm season; services may be suspended in bad weather.
