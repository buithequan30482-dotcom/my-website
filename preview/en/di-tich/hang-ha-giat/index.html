Hà Giắt Cave

Khu phố Hạ Long 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Archaeological
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

A Stone Age cave site in the former Hạ Long commune; its artefacts closely resemble those of the Cái Bèo culture, one of the earliest prehistoric maritime cultures in Vietnam.

OverviewArchaeological value PhotosReviews

Overview

Hà Giắt Cave is in the former Hạ Long commune (now Hạ Long 1 quarter, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 600 m². The heritage list describes it as a dwelling place of Stone Age people.

Archaeological value

According to Quảng Ninh Newspaper, research by the archaeologist Nguyễn Gia Đối shows that the Hà Giắt artefacts resemble those from the Cái Bèo site in raw material, type and flaking technique. This supports the view that the Cái Bèo culture was widespread around the Hạ Long – Bái Tử Long bay area and was among the earliest prehistoric maritime cultures in Vietnam.

Together with the Ba Vũng archaeological site and Soi Nhụ Cave, Hà Giắt helps build up the rich prehistoric picture of Vân Đồn.

Visitor guide

Hà Giắt Cave is an archaeological site that is not open to visitors, and the cave entrance has not yet been located in the project's data. Please do not enter on your own, dig or collect artefacts.
