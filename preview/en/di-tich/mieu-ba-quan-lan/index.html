Bà Shrine (Quan Lạn)

Thôn Quan Lạn 3, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Ảnh minh họa do hệ thống tạo – cần thay ảnh chính thức

A shrine on Quan Lạn honouring Lady Phạm Khanh, a local woman who, according to the heritage inventory, helped the resistance forces against the Mongol-Yuan army; it was restored in 2013.

HistoryArchitectureSignificance PhotosReviews

History

Bà Shrine ("Shrine of the Lady") is in the former Quan Lạn commune (now Quan Lạn 3 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 2020 and covers about 3,781.6 m². According to the heritage list, it honours Lady Phạm Khanh, a local woman who helped the resistance forces fighting the Mongol-Yuan invaders.

Architecture

The shrine was restored in 2013 and includes the main shrine, a pavilion for the Tiger Spirit (ông Hổ), a pavilion for the Green Serpent (Thanh Xà), an outdoor incense pillar, a well and a votive-paper burner.

Significance

Bà Shrine is one of the places of worship that keep alive Quan Lạn's memory of the resistance against the Mongol-Yuan army, alongside the Quan Lạn communal house, the Trần Khánh Dư Shrine and the shrines of the three Phạm brothers. Details of Lady Phạm Khanh's life have not yet been published in official sources.

Visitor guide

The shrine's exact location is not yet in the project's data; ask residents of Quan Lạn 3 village for directions.

Take a high-speed island ferry from Ao Tiên Port (Bến cảng cao cấp Ao Tiên), the departure point for all passenger boats to Vân Đồn's islands since 28 February 2023. Boats to Quan Lạn Island call at either Quan Lạn port or Minh Châu port, on opposite sides of the island about 12 km apart; electric shuttle cars and motorbike rentals are available on the island.

At places of worship, please dress modestly, keep quiet and respect local religious practices.
