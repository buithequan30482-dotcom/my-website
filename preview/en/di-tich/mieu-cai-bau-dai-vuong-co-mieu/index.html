Cái Bầu Shrine (Đại vương Cổ Miếu)

Thôn Vạn Yên 1, Đặc khu Vân Đồn – Historical
Photo of the surrounding area · Báo Quảng Ninh (ảnh: Trần Thanh – CTV, theo chú thích bài)

A Nguyễn-dynasty shrine in Vạn Yên, also known as Đại vương Cổ Miếu ("Ancient Shrine of the Great King"), dedicated to the god of the estuary.

OverviewBeliefs PhotosReviews

Overview

Cái Bầu Shrine, also called Đại vương Cổ Miếu ("Ancient Shrine of the Great King"), is in the former Vạn Yên commune (now Vạn Yên 1 village, Vân Đồn Special Zone). It was inventoried in 1996 and covers about 500 m².

Beliefs

According to the heritage list, the shrine dates from the Nguyễn dynasty and worships the god of the estuary (Thần cửa biển) – a belief closely tied to the lives of coastal communities.

According to the project's survey coordinates, the shrine is quite close to Cái Bầu Pagoda. No published records yet describe its architecture, artefacts or rituals; this page will be updated when the heritage dossier becomes available.

Visitor guide

The site is on Cái Bầu Island, Vân Đồn's largest island, which is reached by road. From Hạ Long, take the Hạ Long – Vân Đồn expressway; Vân Đồn International Airport is also located in the special zone.

At places of worship, please dress modestly, keep quiet and respect local religious practices.
