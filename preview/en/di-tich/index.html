Special National Monument Thang Loi

Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex

Founded in 1149 under King Lý Anh Tông, Vân Đồn was a network of harbours spread across the islands of Bái Tử Long Bay and served for centuries as Đại Việt's gateway to international trade. In 2023 the complex was designated a Special National Monument.

National Monument Quan Lan

Quan Lạn Relic Cluster: Sao Ỏn, Đông Hồ and Cao Sơn Shrines

A national historical and architectural relic cluster on Quan Lạn island, listed in 1990 and linked to the three Phạm brothers, venerated for their role in the 1288 Battle of Vân Đồn. The communal house, pagoda, Đức Ông Shrine and Trần Khánh Dư Shrine now belong to the Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex.

National Monument Ngoc Vung

President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island

Commemorates President Hồ Chí Minh's visit to the soldiers and people of Ngọc Vừng island in November 1962. The site comprises the helicopter landing ground and a memorial area with an exhibition hall, stele house and half-moon pond; it was listed as a national monument in 2023.

National Monument Ha Long (Van Don)

Ba Vũng Archaeological Site

A type site of the Hạ Long culture on Cái Bầu island, both a settlement and a stone-tool workshop of prehistoric people some 4,500–3,000 years ago. Listed as a national monument in 2025.

Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

Ngọc Vừng Militia 12.7 mm Anti-aircraft Position

On Điếm Canh hill on 24 December 1972, the Ngọc Vừng militia platoon, armed with three 12.7 mm anti-aircraft machine guns, shot down the 200th American aircraft over Quảng Ninh. A provincial monument since 2002.

Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine

An 18th-century place of worship honouring the three Phạm brothers, venerated as village guardian deities for their role in the 1288 Battle of Vân Đồn. Its rite on the third day of Tết was revived in 2024 after more than 60 years.

Provincial Monument Cai Rong

King Lý Anh Tông Temple and Đông Trong Cave

A temple in Cái Rồng honouring King Lý Anh Tông, who founded the Vân Đồn estate in 1149; it is said to date from 1172 and was rebuilt on its old foundations in 1993–1997. The listed site also includes Đông Trong Cave, a dwelling and burial place of the Hạ Long culture.

Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

Ngọc Vừng Ancient Citadel

A coastal defence post beside Trường Chinh beach on Ngọc Vừng island. Nguyễn-dynasty records state it was built in 1839 as Tĩnh Hải fort, though locals long called it the Mạc citadel. Only traces of one wall section remain.

Provincial Monument Doan KetRuins – for reference

Khe Mai Resistance Base

A provincial historical monument in Đoàn Kết 1 hamlet, recorded in the inventory as a revolutionary base of the district Party organisation; listed in 2015. Detailed information is being compiled.

Provincial Monument Quan Lan

Quan Lạn Mother Goddess Temple (Đền Mẫu)

A temple to the Three Mother Goddesses on a spit of land jutting into the sea at the eastern gate of Quan Lạn island, preserving a royal decree of 1889 and several antiques. The Mother's anniversary falls on the 3rd day of the 3rd lunar month. Provincial monument since 2023.

Provincial Monument Quan Lan

Trấn Hải Temple (Đền Cậu)

A temple in former Yến Hải hamlet on Quan Lạn island honouring Hoài Văn Hầu Trần Quốc Toản, the young Trần-dynasty general; restored in 2007. In the past it marked the turning point of the Yến Hải village boat race.

Inventoried site Ban Sen

Hang Nhà Trò Cave

A limestone cave on Bản Sen Island, inventoried as a scenic landscape in 1996. The heritage list records Stone Age traces and an unspoilt natural cave landscape.

Inventoried site Ban Sen

Bản Sen Communal House

The communal house of the Bản Sen islanders, first built in 1925 and dedicated to the general Phạm Công Chính; it has been restored as a three-bay building.

Inventoried site Ban SenRuins – for reference

Hang Hoi Cave

A cave in the Bản Sen area, inventoried in 1996. The heritage list records Stone Age traces and its use for storing food and ammunition during the resistance war against the United States.

Inventoried site Cai RongRuins – for reference

Site of the Capture of a US Pilot

The site marking the capture of a US pilot by the Thạch Hà militia in 1966 in the former Cái Rồng township; today only ruins remain.

Inventoried site Cai RongRuins – for reference

Lý Family Temple (Ba Cô Shrine)

The ruins of a local place of worship in Cái Rồng, recorded in the heritage list as founded in the Lý dynasty and connected with King Lý Anh Tông.

Inventoried site Doan KetRuins – for reference

Hà Vực Village Communal House

The ruins of Hà Vực village's communal house (former Đoàn Kết commune), built in 1911 and dedicated to the village guardian deity; traces of the stepped foundation and some brick and tile fragments remain.

Inventoried site Ha Long (Van Don)Ruins – for reference

Soi Nhụ Cave

A Stone Age cave site discovered in the early 1960s, whose name has been given to the "Soi Nhụ culture" – a prehistoric culture that preceded the Cái Bèo and Hạ Long cultures in the Hạ Long – Bái Tử Long bay area.

Inventoried site Ha Long (Van Don)Ruins – for reference

Hà Giắt Cave

A Stone Age cave site in the former Hạ Long commune; its artefacts closely resemble those of the Cái Bèo culture, one of the earliest prehistoric maritime cultures in Vietnam.

Inventoried site Ha Long (Van Don)

Hạ Long Beach (Vân Đồn)

A beach on Bái Tử Long Bay in the former Hạ Long commune on Cái Bầu Island, inventoried as a scenic landscape in 1996. The area includes Bãi Dài (Long Beach), a popular seaside spot near Cái Bầu Pagoda.

Inventoried site Minh Chau

Minh Châu Village Communal House

The communal house of the fishing village of Minh Châu on Quan Lạn Island, which received royal ordinations in 1887 and 1905; its original roof has been lost and a restoration campaign began in 2015.

Inventoried site Minh Chau

Minh Châu Beach

A crescent-shaped beach of fine white sand, clear water and a gently sloping shore on Quan Lạn Island, often ranked among the most beautiful beaches in northern Vietnam; old syzygium (trâm) trees line its edge.

Inventoried site Minh ChauRuins – for reference

Cổng Bà Shrine

A shrine of local folk belief in Minh Châu, recorded in the heritage inventory as dating from the Lý dynasty.

Inventoried site Minh ChauRuins – for reference

Cái Cổng Landing

The remains of an ancient landing of the Vân Đồn trading port system in Minh Châu, dated by the heritage inventory to the Lý dynasty.
