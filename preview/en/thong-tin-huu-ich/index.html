Getting around

Getting to Vân Đồn by air

Vân Đồn International Airport (IATA: VDO) lies within the special zone; it opened on 30 December 2018 and is about 50 km from central Hạ Long. The arrivals hall has taxi desks, hire cars and buses, and taxi fares are posted at the airport. The Hạ Long – Vân Đồn Airport bus serves air passengers only.

Routes vary by season; please check with the airline before booking.

By road: Hạ Long – Vân Đồn Expressway

From Hà Nội, Hải Phòng or Hạ Long, take the Hạ Long – Vân Đồn Expressway to Cái Bầu island, home to the special zone's centre (Cái Rồng), the airport and Ao Tiên Port. Sites on Cái Bầu such as the King Lý Anh Tông Temple, the Ba Vũng archaeological site and the Khe Mai base can be reached by car or motorbike.

Ao Tiên Port – gateway to the islands

Since 28 February 2023 all island passenger boats in Vân Đồn have moved from Cái Rồng harbour to Ao Tiên high-grade port (in former Hạ Long commune); Cái Rồng now mainly serves fishing boats. Covering about 30 ha and able to handle many vessels at once, Ao Tiên serves routes to Minh Châu, Quan Lạn, Thắng Lợi, Ngọc Vừng, Bản Sen (Vân Đồn) and Cô Tô.

Note: this website does not publish fares or departure times, as they change with the season, operator and weather. Please contact Ao Tiên Port or the boat operator directly for the latest timetable before travelling. Holidays and summer weekends are very busy, so book early.

Boat routes to the heritage islands

Vân Đồn has 10 inland waterway routes to its islands. For heritage visitors, the main routes from Ao Tiên Port are:

  • Quan Lạn: boats dock at Quan Lạn or Minh Châu harbour (about 12 km apart on the same island). Suits the “Quan Lạn – Minh Châu” and “Traces of the Vân Đồn Trading Port” routes.
  • Minh Châu: close to Minh Châu beach, the village communal house and the ancient Con Quy and Cái Cổng landings.
  • Ngọc Vừng: boats dock at Cống Yên; an electric cart takes about 15–20 minutes to the commune centre.
  • Thắng Lợi: home to the Cống Đông – Cống Tây sites of the Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex; many require a small boat, so contact local people in advance.
  • Bản Sen: Nhà Trò Cave, Hoi Cave and Bản Sen Communal House; hire a local guide to explore the caves.

Schedules vary with season and weather; please contact the port for the latest timetable.

Getting around the islands

On Quan Lạn – Minh Châu and Ngọc Vừng, electric tourist carts are the usual way to get around; bicycles and motorbikes can also be hired. On Cái Bầu island (Cái Rồng, Hạ Long, Vạn Yên, Đoàn Kết, Đông Xá), cars, taxis and motorbikes are available. Some inventoried sites are remote, along trails or reachable only by boat; ask local people for directions and never enter caves alone.

Accommodation

Staying on the islands

Quan Lạn has a centre with a market, walking street and many hotels, and Minh Châu alone has more than 50 guesthouses and hotels. During the Quan Lạn Communal House Festival (mid-6th lunar month), public holidays and summer weekends accommodation fills up, so book early. This website does not recommend specific establishments.

Health and emergencies

Medical care and emergency numbers

National emergency numbers (free, 24/7):

  • 113 – Police
  • 114 – Rescue and fire services
  • 115 – Medical emergencies

The main medical facility is Vân Đồn Regional General Hospital (under the Quảng Ninh Department of Health, formed by merging the Vân Đồn and Cô Tô health centres); there are also the Cái Bầu and Vân Hải health stations and local health posts. Phone numbers for these facilities are being updated.

On the outer islands, advanced emergency care is limited and depends on the weather; visitors with health conditions should bring their own medication.

Visitor tips

Dress and conduct at places of worship

  • Wear modest clothing (no swimwear, spaghetti straps or very short shorts) when entering communal houses, pagodas, temples, shrines and nghè.
  • Remove hats and speak softly; do not touch statues, ritual objects or royal decrees.
  • Ask the caretaker before photographing altars; do not use flash on ancient artefacts.
  • Place offerings and incense only where designated; burn votive paper only in the designated burner.
  • During festivals, respect the rites and organisers' instructions and make way for processions.
  • At ruins, ancient landings and archaeological sites, do not dig or remove sherds, old coins or stones: they are heritage objects protected by law.

Sea safety and the storm season

  • Always wear a life jacket on boats; use only registered vessels boarding at authorised landings.
  • The storm season on the Quảng Ninh coast is concentrated in summer and autumn (as with Typhoon No. 3 in 2024). If a sea ban or route suspension is announced, postpone your trip and follow the special zone authorities' updates.
  • Some sites lie at the water's edge (Đền Mẫu, ancient landings): watch the tide and keep back from rock edges in heavy seas.
  • Swim only at beaches with lifeguards, stay close to shore and do not swim during thunderstorms.
  • Enter caves only with a local guide, a torch and shoes with good grip.
