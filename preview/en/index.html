Vân Đồn – home of Đại Việt's ancient trading port

Since 1149, the harbours of the Bái Tử Long archipelago have welcomed merchant ships from many lands. Explore the heritage sites, see them on the map and plan your trip to the islands.

Special National Monument Thang Loi

Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex

Founded in 1149 under King Lý Anh Tông, Vân Đồn was a network of harbours spread across the islands of Bái Tử Long Bay and served for centuries as Đại Việt's gateway to international trade. In 2023 the complex was designated a Special National Monument.

National Monument Quan Lan

Quan Lạn Relic Cluster: Sao Ỏn, Đông Hồ and Cao Sơn Shrines

A national historical and architectural relic cluster on Quan Lạn island, listed in 1990 and linked to the three Phạm brothers, venerated for their role in the 1288 Battle of Vân Đồn. The communal house, pagoda, Đức Ông Shrine and Trần Khánh Dư Shrine now belong to the Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex.

National Monument Ngoc Vung

President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island

Commemorates President Hồ Chí Minh's visit to the soldiers and people of Ngọc Vừng island in November 1962. The site comprises the helicopter landing ground and a memorial area with an exhibition hall, stele house and half-moon pond; it was listed as a national monument in 2023.

National Monument Ha Long (Van Don)

Ba Vũng Archaeological Site

A type site of the Hạ Long culture on Cái Bầu island, both a settlement and a stone-tool workshop of prehistoric people some 4,500–3,000 years ago. Listed as a national monument in 2025.

Provincial Monument Cai Rong

King Lý Anh Tông Temple and Đông Trong Cave

A temple in Cái Rồng honouring King Lý Anh Tông, who founded the Vân Đồn estate in 1149; it is said to date from 1172 and was rebuilt on its old foundations in 1993–1997. The listed site also includes Đông Trong Cave, a dwelling and burial place of the Hạ Long culture.

Inventoried site Minh Chau

Minh Châu Beach

A crescent-shaped beach of fine white sand, clear water and a gently sloping shore on Quan Lạn Island, often ranked among the most beautiful beaches in northern Vietnam; old syzygium (trâm) trees line its edge.
