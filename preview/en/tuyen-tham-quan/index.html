Routes

Themed itineraries. Boat travel to the islands depends on timetables and the weather.

Traces of the Vân Đồn Trading Port

2 days, 1 night – Car (Cái Rồng – Ao Tiên); passenger boat Ao Tiên – Quan Lạn; electric cart, bicycle or motorbike on the island
  1. King Lý Anh Tông Temple and Đông Trong Cave
  2. Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex
  3. Cống Cái Landing
  4. Con Quy Landing
  5. Cái Cổng Landing

Quan Lạn – Minh Châu: Communal Houses, Shrines and the Sea

2 days, 1 night – Passenger boat Ao Tiên – Quan Lạn or Ao Tiên – Minh Châu; electric cart, bicycle or motorbike on the island
  1. Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex
  2. Quan Lạn Relic Cluster: Sao Ỏn, Đông Hồ and Cao Sơn Shrines
  3. Quan Lạn Mother Goddess Temple (Đền Mẫu)
  4. Trấn Hải Temple (Đền Cậu)
  5. Vân Sơn Temple and Shrine
  6. Quan Lạn Beach
  7. Minh Châu Village Communal House
  8. Đức Ông Temple (Minh Châu)
  9. Minh Châu Beach

Ngọc Vừng – In the Footsteps of Uncle Hồ

1 day (overnight on the island possible) – Passenger boat Ao Tiên – Ngọc Vừng (Cống Yên landing); electric cart or motorbike on the island
  1. Cống Yên Landing
  2. President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island
  3. Ngọc Vừng Militia 12.7 mm Anti-aircraft Position
  4. Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine
  5. Ngọc Vừng Beach
  6. Ngọc Vừng Ancient Citadel

Vân Đồn in the Resistance Wars

2 days – Car or motorbike on Cái Bầu island; passenger boat Ao Tiên – Ngọc Vừng
  1. Khe Mai Resistance Base
  2. Site of the Capture of a US Pilot
  3. Kế Bào Coal Mine Memorial Site
  4. Hồ Chí Minh Trail at Sea (Vạn Yên)
  5. President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island
  6. Ngọc Vừng Militia 12.7 mm Anti-aircraft Position
