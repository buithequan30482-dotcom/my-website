Vân Đồn in the Resistance Wars

2 days – Car or motorbike on Cái Bầu island; passenger boat Ao Tiên – Ngọc Vừng

A route through places marking the struggles of the people of Vân Đồn. Day one on Cái Bầu island: the Khe Mai resistance base (Đoàn Kết), the spot where militia captured an American pilot in 1966 (Cái Rồng), the Kế Bào coal mine site where workers rose up in 1929, and the departure point of the “ships without numbers” on the Hồ Chí Minh Trail at Sea (Vạn Yên). Day two crosses to Ngọc Vừng for the Hồ Chí Minh Memorial and the anti-aircraft position on Điếm Canh hill.

Some inventoried sites have no visitor facilities and their locations are being updated; please contact the Special Zone's Culture and Society Office before setting out.

Stops

  1. Khe Mai Resistance Base

    Provincial Monument Doan Ket

    Khe Mai Resistance Base (provincial monument) – contact ahead for directions.

  2. Site of the Capture of a US Pilot

    Inventoried site Cai Rong

    Where Thạch Hà militia captured an American pilot in 1966, now a ruin; location being updated.

  3. Kế Bào Coal Mine Memorial Site

    Inventoried site Van Yen

    Kế Bào coal mine (founded 1 September 1888): surviving iron bridge and ventilation shaft.

  4. Hồ Chí Minh Trail at Sea (Vạn Yên)

    Inventoried site Van Yen

    Hồ Chí Minh Trail at Sea at Vạn Yên, departure point of the “ships without numbers”; location being updated.

  5. President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island

    National Monument Ngoc Vung

    Day 2: Hồ Chí Minh Memorial on Ngọc Vừng.

  6. Ngọc Vừng Militia 12.7 mm Anti-aircraft Position

    Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

    The 12.7 mm position on Điếm Canh hill, where the 200th American aircraft over Quảng Ninh was shot down.
