Quan Lạn – Minh Châu: Communal Houses, Shrines and the Sea

2 days, 1 night – Passenger boat Ao Tiên – Quan Lạn or Ao Tiên – Minh Châu; electric cart, bicycle or motorbike on the island

Two days on an island strip some 20 km long, where seafaring villages preserve many layers of belief: Quan Lạn Communal House honouring King Lý Anh Tông and the guardian deity, the shrines of the three Phạm brothers, the Mother Goddess and Trấn Hải temples at the eastern gate, and the Vân Sơn temple-and-shrine complex at Sơn Hào; then over to Minh Châu for its communal house and Đức Ông Temple. In between lie the white-sand beaches of Quan Lạn and Minh Châu. Visitors arriving in the middle of the 6th lunar month can join the Quan Lạn Communal House Festival, a national intangible cultural heritage.

Stops

  1. Vân Đồn Trading Port Complex

    Special National Monument Thang Loi

    Quan Lạn Communal House and Pagoda, Đức Ông Shrine and Trần Khánh Dư Shrine in the commune centre.

  2. Quan Lạn Relic Cluster: Sao Ỏn, Đông Hồ and Cao Sơn Shrines

    National Monument Quan Lan

    Sao Ỏn Shrine by the Mang River, Đông Hồ Shrine and Cao Sơn Shrine.

  3. Quan Lạn Mother Goddess Temple (Đền Mẫu)

    Provincial Monument Quan Lan

    The Mother Goddess Temple on a spit in the sea at the eastern gate.

  4. Trấn Hải Temple (Đền Cậu)

    Provincial Monument Quan Lan

    Trấn Hải Temple (Đền Cậu), near Eo Gió.

  5. Vân Sơn Temple and Shrine

    Inventoried site Quan Lan

    The Vân Sơn temple-and-shrine complex in Sơn Hào hamlet.

  6. Quan Lạn Beach

    Inventoried site Quan Lan

    Quan Lạn beach – rest and swim.

  7. Minh Châu Village Communal House

    Inventoried site Minh Chau

    Minh Châu village communal house.

  8. Đức Ông Temple (Minh Châu)

    Inventoried site Minh Chau

    Đức Ông Temple (Minh Châu), honouring Phạm Công Chính.

  9. Minh Châu Beach

    Inventoried site Minh Chau

    End at Minh Châu beach, a crescent of sand at its best in the morning.

Dashed lines join the stops in order and do not show the actual route.
