Ngọc Vừng – In the Footsteps of Uncle Hồ

1 day (overnight on the island possible) – Passenger boat Ao Tiên – Ngọc Vừng (Cống Yên landing); electric cart or motorbike on the island

A day on Ngọc Vừng, the hero island commune visited by President Hồ Chí Minh in November 1962. From Cống Yên landing, once a staging post of the old trading port, take an electric cart to the Hồ Chí Minh Memorial in the commune centre, climb Điếm Canh hill where the militia downed the 200th American aircraft over Quảng Ninh, visit the communal house and shrine, then head down to Trường Chinh beach and the remains of the old citadel on the south of the island.

Stops

  1. Cống Yên Landing

    Inventoried site Ngoc Vung

    Arrive at Cống Yên landing, a sheltered cove where Lý–Trần merchant ships stopped.

  2. President Hồ Chí Minh Memorial Site on Ngọc Vừng Island

    National Monument Ngoc Vung

    Hồ Chí Minh Memorial: helicopter landing ground, exhibition hall, stele house, banyan tree.

  3. Ngọc Vừng Militia 12.7 mm Anti-aircraft Position

    Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

    The 12.7 mm anti-aircraft position on Điếm Canh hill.

  4. Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine

    Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

    Ngọc Vừng Communal House and Shrine honouring the Phạm brothers.

  5. Ngọc Vừng Beach

    Inventoried site Ngoc Vung

    Trường Chinh beach, nearly 3 km of sand named after General Secretary Trường Chinh.

  6. Ngọc Vừng Ancient Citadel

    Provincial Monument Ngoc Vung

    Remains of the old citadel (Tĩnh Hải fort) by Trường Chinh beach.

Dashed lines join the stops in order and do not show the actual route.
